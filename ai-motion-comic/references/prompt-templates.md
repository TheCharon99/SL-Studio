# Prompt Templates for AI Motion Comics

## Wuxia (武侠) Style

### Scene Prompts

**Snowy Ancient Town:**
```
Cinematic wuxia film style, heavy snowfall in ancient Jiangnan water town, 
stone pathway, traditional Chinese architecture with white walls and black tiles,
ancient archway with Chinese characters, lanterns glowing warm yellow,
mist rising from river, cold blue and white color palette, film grain,
atmospheric perspective, 8k, ultra detailed
```

**Inn Interior:**
```
Cinematic wuxia film style, interior of ancient Chinese inn, warm candlelight,
clay stove with flickering fire, wooden beams and lattice windows,
snow visible through windows, steam rising from tea cups,
traditional Chinese decor, amber and brown tones, atmospheric,
shallow depth of field, film noir lighting
```

**Swordsman Back View:**
```
Cinematic wuxia film style, back view of swordsman in dark teal robes,
bamboo hat dripping with snow water, longjian sword at side,
sitting in corner of dimly lit inn, warm firelight from stove,
mysterious and solitary atmosphere, cool blue shadows,
film grain, 8k, dramatic composition
```

**Mysterious Woman Entry:**
```
Cinematic wuxia film style, heavy snow blowing into inn as wooden door opens,
mysterious woman in white robes silhouetted against blizzard,
holding oil-paper umbrella, face hidden by bamboo hat,
dramatic entrance, slow motion, epic composition,
cold blue and white palette, film grain
```

**Face Close-up:**
```
Extreme close-up of wuxia swordsman's face, bamboo hat casting shadows,
cold determined eyes visible, stubble, single snowflake on cheek,
dramatic side lighting from candlelight, shallow depth of field,
film noir aesthetic, wuxia movie still, 8k
```

**Standoff:**
```
Epic cinematic wuxia film style, wide shot of swordsman and mysterious woman
sitting across from each other at wooden table, clay stove between them,
heavy snow falling outside window, white robes vs dark robes contrast,
tension in the air, snowflakes in candlelight,
slow camera pull back, epic composition,
cold blue and warm amber color grading, 8k
```

## Motion Prompts

For video generation, add motion description:

```
camera slowly zooms in, snow falling heavily, atmospheric
```

```
fire flickering, steam rising, subtle movement, warm lighting
```

```
door opens, snow blows in, figure enters slowly, dramatic
```

```
slow push in towards subject, shallow depth of field
```

```
camera pulls back revealing wider scene, epic composition
```
